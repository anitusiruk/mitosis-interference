# Modern comparator port plan and claim boundaries

The executed reference, head-only, fixed-single, pre-update scoring and resource sensitivities are controlled diagnostic baselines. They do **not** constitute reproductions of the following modern methods. Do not place author-reported benchmark numbers beside our small DistilBERT stress-test numbers as though the experiments were matched.

| Comparator | Primary source | Required compatibility work and validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CABLE | https://www.nature.com/articles/s44387-025-00028-4 ; author code https://github.com/jjul482/CABLE | Eq.10 actual pre/post loss-ratio signal is the closest necessary signal comparator. Preserve its stated update convention and distinguish original SGD lookahead from an AdamW port. The full method includes PPO allocation and class-oriented state information; a scalar-ratio heuristic is an ablation, not CABLE. Match input-label availability, memory, and controller compute; validate each signal against the paper formula before closed-loop runs. |
| SEMA | https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Wang_Self-Expansion_of_Pre-trained_Models_with_Mixture_of_Adapters_for_Continual_CVPR_2025_paper.html | Port distribution descriptor and mixture retrieval to text features, with training-only descriptor updates. Its vision-backbone results do not directly compare to these text streams. Separate faithful original reproduction from text-backbone adaptation and account for descriptor/router parameters. |
| Online-LoRA | https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05663 | Preserve task-free online regularization and expansion logic from author implementation. Inspect loss-based trigger and optimizer reset conventions. Match available stream information; do not supply our evaluator boundaries to the method. |
| SAFM | https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.37/ | Full search includes explicit task episodes and pseudo-replay. A task-boundary-informed port belongs in a separate information condition. Empty adapter selection is not literal discarded-supervision DEFER. Include search and replay compute in resources. |
| HESTIA | https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/le26a.html | Preserve linearized adaptation and density-based retrieval, not merely a nearest-centroid router. Check retained distributions, routing state and head handling, then match text features/labels and stored resources. |
| FiUni | https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27070v1 | Batch-level Fisher/subspace construction and reuse/expansion are close comparators. Inspect author implementation and K-FAC approximation before porting. Include factor storage and decomposition time. Do not call a gradient-cosine threshold a FiUni reproduction. |
| TORA | https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01702v1 | Its incoming-task fingerprint is obtained after one epoch. That information condition differs from a single incoming-batch decision. Report task-episode reproduction separately from any batch adaptation; retain SVD construction and threshold provenance. |

For each executed port save the exact author commit, environment, model/data revisions, split rules, seed/order, optimizer semantics, budget, and an equation-level verification. Reproduce at least one small author setting before treating a text adaptation as a competitive baseline. Keep method selection and tuning on development seeds; reserve new seeds/orders and final held-out data only after the complete learner, router and resource protocol are frozen.

Submission-facing scope is still undecided. The strongest current evidence concerns classifier reset confounding, the difference between initial predictor changes and prospective learning gain, and the gap between oracle specialization and label-free deployment. Whether these form a sufficiently useful and distinctive contribution requires the outstanding comparator and larger-regime work; no acceptance or novelty score is assigned.
