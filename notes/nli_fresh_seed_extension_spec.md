# Fresh-seed NLI allocation-control extension

Declared after the complete seed-2026 learnability pilot and before any outcome from this extension. This is a development extension, not an externally registered or untouched-test confirmation. It does not replace the original unfinished 20-cell factorial queue.

## Question and frozen design
Does enabling LoRA learning change the benefit or decisions of the classifier-bearing controller once a natural shared-label stream is demonstrably learnable? A good fixed learner alone does not answer this question. The contribution candidate is an auditable attribution procedure and its empirical limits, not a new theorem about optimizer state or classifier importance.

Use fresh initialization/controller seeds 31101, 31102, 31103, 31104, 31105. Use the exact saved version-2 5760-example fit corpus, 576-example development corpus, and two existing orders, with their recorded hashes. Training examples are deliberately fixed across seeds. These seeds measure initialization/controller variability conditional on this corpus; they are not five independent datasets. Both orders must complete before a seed contributes to an interval.

Keep the verified DistilBERT bytes, three-label space, 256-token pair encoding, rank 8, alpha 16, zero LoRA dropout, original classifier-stack dropout, AdamW settings and 360 incoming batches from the learnability pilot. Freeze the common rate at the pilot-selected 8e-4 for every condition. This common-rate intervention is not separately tuned head-only performance or a claim of competitive optimality.

For every seed and order run six conditions: (1) full classifier-plus-LoRA controller, (2) classifier-only controller with LoRA frozen, (3) full fixed single package updated on every batch, (4) classifier-only fixed single package updated on every batch, (5) full fixed single package updated only at the incoming steps accepted by condition 1, and (6) classifier-only fixed single package updated only at the incoming steps accepted by condition 2. Conditions 5 and 6 are diagnostic controls yoked to the corresponding controller's saved accepted-update mask; they cannot run before that controller finishes. Total: 60 complete trajectories. No automatic launch queue is authorized by this specification.

Use the existing fixed-total-reservoir controller: maximum eight packages, 512 total stored examples, 64-example probe, original feasibility/utility rules. Keep the entire controller source byte-identical to its recorded original version. Record actual stored examples, allocated slots, trainable parameters, optimizer bytes, peak GPU memory, trial updates, live updates, evaluation cost and wall time. The conditions share incoming examples and specified memory caps, not equal parameter count or equal computation. Never describe this as an equal-compute comparison. Yoked controls isolate update timing/count only; they do not isolate allocation from routing, package capacity, or reset history.

## Outcomes and analysis
Predefine frozen-feature centroid routing as the primary deployable rule. Fit its centroids only to the training reservoirs, never development labels, genres or query groups. Preserve last-active and uniform-probability routing as secondary rules without selecting the best afterward. Fixed-single rules must agree exactly. No oracle task selection is a primary result.

Primary endpoint: final development accuracy averaged equally over the three genres. Primary contrast: (full controller minus full always-update single) minus (head-only controller minus head-only always-update single), first paired within seed/order and then averaged over orders within seed. This is the interaction of two complete algorithm interventions, not a universal causal fraction attributable to LoRA. Report all four means, both component differences, the interaction, and every seed/order. Secondary outcomes include yoked differences, losses, per-class accuracy, retention from earlier segment ends, decisions and resource costs.

Report a descriptive paired Student interval using five independent training seeds; do not treat examples, orders, tasks or checkpoints as independent training replicates. Show leave-one-seed-out sensitivity and the full paired differences. Five seeds cannot support a two-sided 5% exact sign-randomization rejection with only 32 sign patterns. Do not use an interval covering zero as equivalence, or call adaptive update feasibility a 95% no-forgetting guarantee. Secondary endpoints and routers are descriptive and not multiplicity-adjusted confirmation.

## Integrity and compute gates before the grid
Implement and inspect the runner before any fresh-seed learning. Require identical initial per-example logits across the six conditions of each seed, exact 256-token smoke checks, unchanged data/source/spec hashes, finite full-space logits, saved per-example predictions, and independent metric recomputation. Prove head-only LoRA tensors remain bitwise unchanged. Check controller trial-state restoration including optimizer/RNG/modes, reservoir budget after every action, and checkpoint reload predictions. Preserve incomplete or failed attempts in fresh paths; stop on an integrity failure rather than changing the recipe invisibly.

First run a separately identified 32-step seed-2026 timing/integrity prefix for each controller architecture, with no use of its learning performance to change rates, data, thresholds or conditions. Admit full jobs only with conservative projected runtime below 15 minutes and peak allocated memory below 8 GiB on the observed 24 GiB RTX 4090. Each job retains a 23-minute internal deadline and 25-minute process-group cap. Launch one job at a time, require an idle GPU, and record every timing attempt. A failed compute gate requires a documented scheduling/protocol decision before any new fresh-seed outcome; it never licenses silently shrinking the study or selecting favorable cells.

The repeatedly viewed development corpus remains a limitation. A separately frozen final evaluation and complete comparable published-method reproduction remain necessary before broad or competitive claims. No official validation/test evaluation is launched by this extension specification.
